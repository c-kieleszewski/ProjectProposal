1. Background and Motivation

DDoS attacks have become more common and problematic in recent years.
I have been playing video games since I was 10 years old and remember the times of how common and normalized DDoS became in my earlier years of gaming.
What started off as gamers DDoSing each other to win money wagers against each other turned into full
scale attacks of large servers that hosted video game companies’ games, potentially losing them millions of dollars.
DDoSing will get more serious and problematic if greater security practices aren’t involved.
Some challenges I may incur include having a very basic understanding of what a DDoS attack is.
With how much cybersecurity and threats are evolving, it may be difficult to find relevant information.
Objectives:

  • Understand how DDoS attacks work at a high level
  • Understand common attacks used by hackers in today's landscape
  • Understand how special security practices are used in today’s landscape
  • Understand how the largest DDoS attacks work
  • Improve ways to combat DDoS attacks
